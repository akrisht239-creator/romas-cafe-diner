Since 2016

Nine years of
second helpings.

Roma's began as a small café near Ravidas Gate with a short menu and long conversations. It grew the way good cafés do — one regular at a time. Students claimed the window seats, families filled the long tables, and the bookshelf kept expanding until it became part of the furniture.

Today the kitchen travels further than most — North Indian curries one hour, lasagna the next — but the idea has not moved an inch: cook honestly, serve warmly, and never rush anyone out the door.
